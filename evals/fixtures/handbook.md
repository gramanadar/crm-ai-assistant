# Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 30 days under policy POL-REF-30. Refunds for a failed payment are handled by billing. The refund is issued to the original payment method.

# Shipping

Standard shipping takes five business days. Express shipping takes two business days under code SHIP-EXP-2. The warehouse closes at 18:00 local time.

# Warranty

Product SKU-ACME-1 includes a 12 month warranty. Warranty claims need the order number and a short description of the fault.

# Support hours

The support desk is open from 09:00 to 17:00 ET on weekdays. Emergency outages are handled by the on-call engineer.

# PTO

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off each year. PTO requests are submitted to the manager at least five days ahead.

# Expenses

Expenses over 500 dollars need manager approval before they are reimbursed. Meals under 75 dollars do not need a receipt.

# Remote work

Employees spend three days a week in the office. The other two days may be worked from home.

# Security

Report phishing messages to security@example.com. Do not forward suspected malware to coworkers.

# Billing cycle

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month. A duplicate charge should be reported to billing with the invoice number.

# Account access

Password resets start on the account page. Changing the email address on a profile is an account request, not a technical outage.
